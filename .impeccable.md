## Design Context

### Users
The Song Room is for independent artists, songwriters, producers, bands, and their invited collaborators. They use it while reviewing songs remotely, comparing versions, leaving timestamped feedback, and agreeing what should happen next.

### Brand Personality
Collaborative, passionate, energetic, and excited. The product should make creative progress feel shared and immediate while keeping instructions and feedback clear.

### Aesthetic Direction
A focused, music-led interface with a warm dark palette, strong editorial display type, and restrained red accents. It should feel like a purposeful creative workspace rather than generic project-management software. Preserve the established visual system and support both desktop and mobile use.

### Design Principles
- Keep the song and the shared creative process at the centre of each screen.
- Make state, ownership, and the next useful action immediately clear.
- Use energetic emphasis selectively so important moments feel exciting without making routine work noisy.
- Write direct, human copy that explains what happened and how the user can recover.
- Preserve accessible contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and readable layouts across screen sizes.
