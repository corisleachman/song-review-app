# The Song Room SEO brand context

## Product

The Song Room is a shared workspace where artists, producers, songwriters and bands move songs from first demo to finished track.

Each song has its own room where collaborators can upload versions, leave timestamped comments on the waveform, organise feedback, record decisions and keep everyone working from the latest version.

## Core positioning

- Primary promise: Keep songs moving from first demo to finished track.
- Brand idea: The shared space where songs evolve.
- Supporting line: Where songs get finished.

The Song Room is not generic cloud storage, generic project management or a replacement for a DAW. It is a collaboration workflow designed specifically around how recorded songs develop.

## Audiences

### Primary
Independent artists, songwriters and producers who regularly exchange demos and work remotely.

### Secondary
Bands that need one organised place for versions, feedback and decisions between sessions.

### High-value user
Producers who work across several artists or projects. Their invitations can introduce new collaborators to the product.

## Product capabilities that may be stated

- Upload and organise song versions.
- Identify the latest version.
- Leave timestamped feedback on the waveform.
- Keep comments and decisions attached to the song.
- Invite collaborators into a shared song room.
- Track actions and next steps.

Do not invent capabilities, integrations, customer numbers, testimonials, prices or performance claims.

## Voice

- Music-native, calm and knowledgeable.
- Direct and useful rather than promotional.
- Premium and editorial, but never pretentious.
- British English.
- Vary sentence length.
- Avoid generic AI phrases, corporate jargon and exaggerated claims.
- Do not use em dashes.

## Editorial principles

1. Answer the reader's question before mentioning the product.
2. Give practical steps, examples and workflows musicians can use immediately.
3. Use realistic music-making situations rather than generic workplace examples.
4. Mention The Song Room only where it naturally solves a problem discussed in the article.
5. Treat competing tools fairly. Explain workflow limitations without attacking brands.
6. Never invent statistics, quotes, research or customer stories.
7. Prefer specific advice over broad inspiration.
8. End with one relevant call to action.

## SEO requirements

Each article must include:

- One clear primary search query.
- A concise SEO title.
- A meta description no longer than 155 characters.
- One H1 and descriptive H2 and H3 headings.
- A direct answer near the start.
- Practical steps or a structured method.
- A short FAQ section where useful.
- Natural internal-link suggestions.
- A relevant product call to action.
- Article JSON-LD generated by the publishing script.
