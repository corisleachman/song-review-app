Song Room · Email Suite Preview

All 13 emails in the TSR v3 brand: near-black canvas, straw (acid-yellow) accent, off-white text, hard 1px borders, zero border-radius. Rendered with email-safe HTML (tables + inline styles). Display type uses a heavy web-safe fallback because email clients can't load ThunderLC — the wordmark keeps the brand feel via weight and letter-spacing. Emails 1–4 exist today (2 Resend routes + 2 Supabase auth); 5–13 are proposed. Lifecycle nudges (6, 7, 9, 10, 11) need a scheduler + eligibility checks; marketing (12, 13) go out as Resend broadcasts. Marketing / lifecycle emails carry an unsubscribe footer.
