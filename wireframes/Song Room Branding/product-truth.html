What The Song Room is

The Song Room is where collaborators agree on what a song needs — by leaving feedback pinned to the exact moment it's about.

It's built for artists, bands and producers passing versions back and forth. Every note lives on the waveform, every decision becomes a tracked action, and nothing gets lost between takes.

What The Song Room is not
  • A DAW or audio editor
  • A file storage drive
  • A streaming or distribution platform
  • A generic chat or project-management tool
  • A social network for musicians

If it starts behaving like one, it's drifting.

The primary promise
"I can point at the exact second of the song, and we both know precisely what needs to change."
If feedback ever drifts back to vague and untracked, that's the problem to fix.
The most important actions

Everything else is secondary.

Design principles
Principle 1
Feedback lives on the waveform
Context is never optional. A note belongs on the audio it's about, not in a box beside it.
Principle 2
Precision over volume
The right note at the right second beats a long paragraph of vague description.
Principle 3
Nothing actionable gets lost
Any comment worth acting on becomes a decision you can track across versions.
Principle 4
Listening mode vs. work mode
The dashboard is for browsing and continuous play. The song page is for focused review.
Principle 5
The song is the hero
Colour comes from the artwork, the interface gets out of the way of the audio.
