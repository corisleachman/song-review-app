The Problem

Songs
get lost
in transit.

  • 01

    Versions scatter. V1, V2_final, V2_FINAL_actual, V3_USE_THIS. Nobody knows which file is current.

  • 02

    Feedback gets buried. Notes sit in voice messages, texts, and email threads. The detail disappears.

  • 03

    Decisions evaporate. You know you agreed to change the bridge. Nobody wrote it down. It never happened.

  • 04

    Moments get missed. "It was around the chorus" isn't the same as a pin at exactly 1:32.

One
Room.
Every

The Song Room keeps every version of your track in one place, with timestamped comments pinned to the exact moment you mean, a clear record of what was said, what needs doing, and where the song is going.

How
It Works

Four steps. One room. The full story of every song.

01

Upload a Song

Add your track to The Song Room. Give it a title. It lives here now — every version of it, forever.

02

Add Versions

Upload a new mix, revision, or take. Each version is numbered and labelled. The full history stays visible.

Click the waveform at any point to leave a note. Your collaborator gets a direct link to that exact moment.
